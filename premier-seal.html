The Six Vetting Requirements

The same trust promise as the Gold Seal.

Premier Seal cannot be purchased. Every provider must meet all six requirements before any credential is issued — and re-verify annually.

01

Valid business license

For your specific service category, current and verified. Applies to every regulated service category in New Jersey.

02

General liability insurance

Minimum $500,000 per occurrence for adjacent trade providers. Certificate on file with HomeGuard before any listing is activated. Current verification required annually.

03

Background check

Owner and all employees who enter homeowner properties. Conducted and paid for by HomeGuard. Full background verification completed before credential is issued.

04

Three verified customer references

Contacted and confirmed by HomeGuard directly. Not self-reported. Three actual homeowners who can speak to your work.

05

Premier Seal Academy — eight-module training

Three-week structured curriculum delivered through Success Institute of New Jersey. Two sessions per week. Google Classroom reinforcement between sessions. Covers safety and compliance, customer communication, project scoping, quality standards, environmental stewardship, business fundamentals, equipment operation, and customer privacy. The same commitment to excellence as the Gold Seal Academy — purpose-built for your trade.

06

HomeGuard Standards Agreement

Signed commitment to conduct and quality standards. Annual re-verification required. The credential follows your ongoing performance — not just a passed test.

The Premier Seal Standard

What you commit to. What homeowners expect.

The Premier Seal is bilateral. Both providers and homeowners commit to the standard. This is what makes the credential meaningful.

Providers Commit To
  • ✓Responding to homeowner inquiries within 36 hours
  • ✓Sending only background-checked personnel to homeowner properties
  • ✓Maintaining active insurance and license throughout membership
  • ✓Completing services as scoped and agreed — no surprise additions
  • ✓Maintaining the conduct and quality standards of the HomeGuard Standards Agreement
  • ✓Annual re-verification and training module completion

Providers who do not meet the standard after issuance are subject to the same three-tier monitoring system as Gold Seal contractors — Advisory, Intervention, Suspension. The Premier Seal can be revoked.
