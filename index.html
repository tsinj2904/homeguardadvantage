For Homeowners

Every home service you need.
One call, one click, and it's done.

Before any provider touches your home — they have been trained, tested, vetted, and certified to a standard that no other platform requires. Here is exactly what HomeGuard does on your behalf.

What We Do Before Anyone Arrives
  • ✓ Six-week rigorous business training — every Gold Seal contractor completes NJ consumer law, financial management, estimating, and business operations before earning their credential
  • ✓ Comprehensive final exam — objective, standardized, cannot be purchased. 90%+ earns Gold Seal. The same bar for everyone
  • ✓ Background check and financial verification — every contractor, every provider, before any credential is issued
  • ✓ License and insurance verified — current NJ HIC registration and minimum $1M general liability confirmed on file
  • ✓ Premier Seal providers vetted — every landscaper, cleaner, pool company, and home service provider is background checked, insured, reference-verified, and trained before listing
What Protects You During the Project
  • ✓ Standard protective contract — in place before a single nail is driven. Scope, price, timeline, and warranty terms all documented
  • ✓ Milestone-based financing — your funds are released as verified work is completed. You never pay for work that has not been done
  • ✓ Unbiased estimate first — before any contractor is involved, HomeGuard tells you what your project should realistically cost in your NJ county
  • ✓ Ongoing performance monitoring — every certified provider is monitored after credentialing. Advisory, Intervention, Suspension. The credential can be revoked
What Backs You If Something Goes Wrong
  • ✓ Written arbitration backstop — Success Institute of NJ is the named arbitrator on every project. You are never alone in a dispute
  • ✓ Rescue contractor protocol — if a contractor abandons your project, HomeGuard assigns a certified replacement and pursues recovery on your behalf

How HomeGuard works.

Whether you are a homeowner protecting your investment or a contractor building your business, the process is straightforward.

01

Submit your project

Fill out the HomeGuard project portal with your scope, timeline, and budget range. No commitment required — just the details we need to get started.

02

Receive an accurate estimate

Success Institute reviews your project and provides an independent, unbiased rough estimate — so you know what fair pricing looks like before anyone shows up at your door.

03

Get matched and protected

We match you with a HomeGuard Gold Seal certified contractor and put a standard protective contract in place. Milestone payments, arbitration clause, and full backstop included.

Complete the certification

Six modules covering NJ consumer law, financial management, estimating, client communication, and scaling — plus a comprehensive final exam and background check.

03

Earn your Gold Seal
