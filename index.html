What "Backed" Actually Means — Precisely

HomeGuard is not an insurance company. We do not guarantee every outcome. What we guarantee is that the right protections are in place before anything goes wrong — and that you are never alone if it does.

✓
Your payments are protected at every stage.
Funds held in escrow and released only on verified milestone completion. Same rhythm as paying in thirds — except your money never goes directly to the contractor until the work is done.
✓
Your contractor carries $1M in verified insurance.
Every Gold Seal contractor's insurance is confirmed on file before any project begins. Covered losses are the contractor's insurer's responsibility.
✓
If your contractor abandons — the project does not stop.
HomeGuard freezes remaining escrow and assigns a certified replacement contractor. The money goes toward finishing your project — not the contractor who walked off.
✓
Every dispute has a neutral referee.
Success Institute of New Jersey — an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit — is the named arbitrator on every HomeGuard contract. No financial stake in any outcome.
