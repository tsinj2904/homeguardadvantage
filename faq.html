No other platform does all three.

Protection and Recourse
HomeGuard has a three-part response. Step in immediately — you are not left to navigate a dispute alone. HomeGuard contacts the contractor and facilitates resolution. Protect your funds — milestone financing means money is not released for work that has not been completed and verified. Rescue contractor protocol — if a contractor abandons your project HomeGuard assigns a certified replacement contractor to complete the work. We pursue recovery from the original contractor on your behalf.
Every HomeGuard project includes a written arbitration clause designating Success Institute of New Jersey as the neutral arbitrator in any dispute. This means if you and your contractor cannot resolve a disagreement you have a documented, binding process for resolution without going to court. The arbitration backstop is included in every HomeGuard standard contract at no additional cost to you.
The project is divided into defined phases agreed upon in the contract before work begins — framing, rough electrical, rough plumbing, drywall, finishes, completion. When each phase is completed and verified, payment for that phase is released. You never fund more than one phase ahead of completed work. A contractor cannot receive payment for phase three until phase two is verified complete. This protects your money at every stage of the project.
The Gold Seal Academy
Licensed NJ home improvement contractors — general contractors, electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians — who are actively operating in NJ and want to elevate their business standards, distinguish themselves from competitors, and access a pre-qualified homeowner project pipeline. You must hold a current NJ Home Improvement Contractor registration to apply.
Six weeks. The Master Builder Academy is delivered through Google Classroom — self-paced within the weekly module schedule. Each of the six modules ends with a competency quiz at 80% pass threshold with one retake per module. The Module 5 business plan is reviewed and approved by Dr. Alonso before you sit the final exam. The final comprehensive exam is timed and open note — 90% or above earns the Gold Seal, 80%–89% earns HomeGuard Certified. Background check and financial verification are completed before any credential is issued.
Both tiers require the same six-week Academy and the same background check and financial verification. The difference is performance on the final comprehensive exam. Gold Seal — 90% or above — the distinction tier. Priority placement in the homeowner portal, first right of refusal on incoming projects, Gold Seal for truck and marketing materials. HomeGuard Certified — 80% to 89% — the entry tier. Standard portal listing and lead access. Both are earned — neither is purchased.
The Gold Seal tells every homeowner who sees it that you earned a credential through a process that cannot be purchased. You get priority placement in the HomeGuard homeowner portal, access to pre-qualified project referrals, a standard contract framework that protects you legally, and the Gold Seal for your truck, website, and marketing materials. More importantly — the Gold Seal separates you from every other contractor competing for the same clients. It is the only credential in NJ home services that cannot be bought. Only earned.
The founding cohort is the first 20 licensed NJ contractors to enroll. Tuition is $3,000 — the founding cohort rate only. After 20 spots fill the standard rate is $5,000. Founding cohort members also have their lead referral fees waived on their first three completed HomeGuard projects. Apply at homeguardadvantage.com before the cohort fills.
Leads and Network
Once certified you receive an active listing in the HomeGuard homeowner portal. When a homeowner submits a project matching your trade and geography, HomeGuard notifies you. Lead fees are tiered by project size — small projects $150, medium $300, large $500. Founding cohort members have their first three project lead fees waived. Gold Seal contractors receive priority placement — their profiles surface first in homeowner matches.
Gold Seal and HomeGuard Certified contractors renew annually at $5,000. Renewal includes continuing education credits pending NJ Board of Home Improvement approval, performance record review, background check refresh, and financial standing confirmation. The monthly network fee is $200 — which maintains your active portal listing and lead access year-round.
HomeGuard has a three-tier response system. Advisory — HomeGuard contacts you with the complaint and gives you an opportunity to resolve it directly. Intervention — HomeGuard facilitates a formal resolution process including the written arbitration backstop with Success Institute as neutral arbitrator. Suspension — if the issue is not resolved or represents a significant standard violation, your credential is suspended pending review. The goal is always resolution — not punishment. Quality contractors benefit from a platform that holds everyone to the same standard.
The Premier Seal Program
Any established NJ home service business outside of licensed renovation contracting. This includes landscaping, lawn care, cleaning services, tree removal and trimming, pool maintenance and installation, snow removal, pest control, restoration and remediation, fencing, outdoor kitchens, security camera installation, smart home installation, senior proofing, baby proofing, and every other home service category homeowners need. If you serve homeowners and you are not a licensed general contractor or licensed trade — the Premier Seal is for you.
Six requirements — all verified before listing: Valid business license for your specific service category. General liability insurance — minimum $1,000,000 per occurrence, certificate on file with HomeGuard. Background check — owner and all employees who enter homeowner properties. Three verifiable customer references — contacted and confirmed by HomeGuard. Three-module training — ethics, business standards, and customer service — delivered through Google Classroom. HomeGuard Standards Agreement — signed commitment to HomeGuard conduct and quality standards.
Visit homeguardadvantage.com and submit a Premier Seal provider application. You will need your business license, proof of general liability insurance, three verifiable customer references, and owner background check authorization. Once your application is reviewed and verified you complete the three-module HomeGuard training program and sign the HomeGuard Standards Agreement. Annual listing fee is $500–$1,000 depending on service category plus $100 per month portal access fee.
The Premier Seal tells every homeowner who sees it that you have been vetted, insured, background checked, and trained to a standard that no directory or review platform requires. You get an active listing in the HomeGuard homeowner portal with exclusive pre-qualified leads — not shared with five competitors simultaneously. You can display the Premier Seal on your truck, website, and marketing materials. And you stand apart from every unvetted operator in your market who is competing for the same clients without meeting any standard.
Every Premier Seal provider re-verifies annually to maintain their listing. Re-verification confirms that your business license remains current, your insurance has not lapsed, and your standing with HomeGuard remains in good order. The annual re-verification fee is $250. This is what separates the Premier Seal from a one-time background check that any directory offers — the standard is maintained year over year, not just at initial application.
When a homeowner submits a service request matching your category and geography, HomeGuard notifies you. Lead fees are tiered by job size — small jobs $25, medium $50, large $75. Leads are exclusive — not sent to multiple competing providers simultaneously. The homeowner specifically chose a Premier Seal vetted provider over an unvetted one before the match is made. These are pre-qualified leads, not cold inquiries.
