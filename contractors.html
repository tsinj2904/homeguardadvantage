How You Get Projects

Not a directory. Not a bidding war. A match.

Here is exactly how a HomeGuard project goes from homeowner submission to your phone ringing.

Step 1

Homeowner submits their project

The homeowner completes a detailed project submission — scope, location, timeline, budget. HomeGuard reviews it, prepares an independent market-rate estimate, and delivers it to the homeowner before any contractor is involved.

Step 2

HomeGuard matches the project

HomeGuard reviews the trade required, the project location, and available Gold Seal certified contractors in that category. One Gold Seal contractor is matched directly. No auction. No competitive bidding. The homeowner receives one certified recommendation.

Step 3

You receive a complete project brief

You receive a project brief through the HomeGuard portal containing the full scope of work, the independent estimate, the homeowner's confirmed budget, the project address, and the timeline. Everything you need before the first conversation.

Step 4

You contact the homeowner to close

You have 36 hours to respond through the HomeGuard portal. The homeowner already knows what the project costs and has been matched to you specifically. You are not competing. You are not educating. You are closing.

Gold Seal — Exclusive Direct Matching

When a homeowner submits a project HomeGuard matches them to one Gold Seal contractor in that trade and geography. No other contractor sees that lead. You have exclusive access. The homeowner is yours to close.

HomeGuard Certified — Directory and Referral

Certified contractors are listed in the HomeGuard directory where homeowners can find and contact you directly. You also receive referrals when no Gold Seal contractor is available in your trade or geography. Score 90%+ at your six-month retake and your matching access upgrades immediately.

Your Journey

From application to your first project — here is exactly what happens.

No surprises. No hidden steps. This is the complete path from the day you apply to the day your first HomeGuard project brief arrives.

1

Submit your application

Complete the application form at the bottom of this page. You will provide your NJ HIC license number, insurance carrier information, trade category, and service area. HomeGuard reviews every application before admission to the Academy.

Timeline: Same week

2

Background check and vetting

HomeGuard conducts a comprehensive background check — included in your enrollment fee, paid for by HomeGuard. Your NJ HIC license is verified as current and in good standing. Your insurance is confirmed on file. Your financial history is reviewed. This vetting protects every homeowner in the network — and every other contractor in it.

Timeline: 3 to 5 business days

3

Enroll in the Gold Seal Academy

Six weeks. Two sessions per week. Two hours each. Delivered by Success Institute of New Jersey faculty with real contracting operations experience. You receive Google Classroom access for curriculum reinforcement between sessions. The founding cohort rate is $3,000. Standard rate is $5,000. 20 spots only.

Timeline: 6 weeks

4

Sit the comprehensive final exam

The exam covers all six weeks of curriculum. It is objective, standardized, and the same for every contractor regardless of trade or experience level. The state requires 70% to issue a contractor license. HomeGuard requires 70% just to earn the entry-level credential. Score 70% to 89% — HomeGuard Certified. Score 90% or above — Gold Seal. Below 70% — retake after six months, same as NJ state licensing standard.

Timeline: End of week 6

5

Receive your credential

Your Gold Seal or HomeGuard Certified credential is issued by Success Institute of New Jersey. Your portal listing goes live. Your seal files are delivered — full color, reversed white, and simplified small formats for your truck, website, business cards, and yard signs. Annual renewal at $5,000 keeps your credential active and your background check current.

Timeline: Within 48 hours of exam results

6

Your first project brief arrives

Gold Seal contractors receive exclusive direct project matches — one contractor, one project, no competition. Your project brief contains the full scope of work, independent estimate, homeowner budget confirmation, project address, and timeline. The homeowner already knows what the project costs. You have 36 hours to respond through the portal. You are not competing. You are closing.
